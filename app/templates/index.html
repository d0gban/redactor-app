How it works

  1. Paste your prompt. Secrets, contact details, IDs and URLs are detected as you type.
  2. Add names, companies or projects under Always redact, or select text and press Redact selection.
  3. Click any token in the safe prompt to keep the original value. Click again to redact it.
  4. Copy the safe prompt and send it to your AI tool.
  5. Paste the AI's reply in Restore reply to put the real values back.
